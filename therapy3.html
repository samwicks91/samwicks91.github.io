Therapy? Pt3

Now that we've got the how out of the way, let's look at the why. Or more to the point, the why me?

First published: -- October 2026

Between May 2019 and May 2024, my life changed radically. After looking back through the things that I have experienced throughout my life, with all the insane, toxic shit that has occured, this might have been the most turbulent, cyclonic and transformative years that I have experienced. The sheer insanity of saying that is really something. As someone who really doesn't want to be involved with drama, even tangentially, I really to have a knack of finding myself in dramatic (and quite often melodramatic) situations. This is not to place blame elsewhere, I wouldn't feel comfortable doing that. I bear responsibility for getting myself involved in bad situations by neglecting my own agency and ability to change course, whether this is a learned, comfortable pattern of behaviour that I have leaned on for many years.

Now that I am in a more settled place, I felt it was probably time to unpack just exactly what the fuck has been going on. I genuinely feel like my life is like an arc of an Eastenders character, a truly weird and wonderful display of how to conspire to find myself in the most awkward and painful situations like torturous, karmic penance for some previous life that I spent doing something terrible. That being said, I don't want this to be too much of a pity party, I have enjoyed much of my time in this period and in fact some of the most wonderful experiences of my life have happened in this period, that just doesn't quite fit the remit of what this post is about so I might not always give these moments the time that they deserve. The good, unfortunately, doesn't register with the depressed mind as strongly as the bad. What fun.

Anyway, if you have read the original post from 2019 and the other one that I posted here recently, they you will know the form: I'm going to go into some detail about just what has happened and how I dealt with it or just how I felt about it and how I feel about it now. Again, it may read as a sanitised version of what I might say in real life, I'm not intending this to be the case but I am looking at writing this in the most direct way that I possibly can, even if what I talk about may call for more emotive language, I will tend towards getting things across in a factual manner. I will write about real people, who I interact with often in my daily life still and as such, I will not name names and I will address events and moments in ways that I feel would be least harmful to anyone mentioned. If you know me, or work out who anyone here is and fancy speaking up about what's inside this blog post, just remember that you probably have shit about you that you certainly wouldn't want people to bring up because it makes you look bad, just because I may or may not like a particular person doesn't mean that you get to criticise them, keep your nasty little thoughts to yourself.
